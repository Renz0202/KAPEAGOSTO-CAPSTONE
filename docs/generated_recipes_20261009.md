## How quantities affect inventory

All quantities below apply to **one serving**. POS multiplies them by the ordered quantity, combines shared materials, checks availability at checkout, and deducts stock when a paid pending order is marked Done. A second completion cannot deduct again. Pending orders use the current saved recipes; completed orders retain their existing consumption history.

Automatic packaging provides one matching cup, lid, and regular straw or hot stirrer. Blended and jelly recipes explicitly use a wide straw instead of the regular straw. Generated recipes include one napkin per drink and one sleeve for hot drinks; these optional consumables can be removed by clearing their recipe quantities.

## Assumptions to review

- Coffee quantities are dry beans in g, not espresso output. Water used for brewing, dilution, or preparation is not inventory-tracked.
- Tea Base is ready-to-serve brewed tea measured in ml. A concentrated tea extract needs different quantities.
- Matcha, Milo, frappe base, and jelly are dry powders in g. Jelly quantities allocate dry powder from a batch, not the finished jelly weight. Check the product's batch yield.
- Ube flavor, syrups, sauces, honey, milk, and cream use the existing ml units. Ube jam, cheese foam powder, or fruit puree may require different materials and quantities.
- Heavy cream is measured before whipping. Biscoff biscuits and spread remain separate.
- Decemberberry Matcha assumes strawberry; Matcha Lava assumes white chocolate; Berry Season Fiz assumes strawberry and blueberry. Confirm these signature flavors.
- Dark Java follows the current Non-Coffee category and has no espresso. Choco Blanco follows the Coffee category and includes beans. Mayo Milo is milk-based in every size, even though its 22oz item currently has a different category.
- Oat milk is available for a separate recipe or variant; these starter recipes use fresh milk. No automatic per-order milk substitution has been added.

Representative references were used to check general proportions: [Breville's 18-22 g espresso guidance](https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-dial-in-espresso.html), [Monin's 16oz iced latte example](https://monin.us/products/zero-cal-iced-caramel-latte), and [Monin's 16oz fruit tea example](https://monin.us/products/strawberry-tea). The individual menu formulas and larger serving amounts are generated estimates.
